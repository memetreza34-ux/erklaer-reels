WRITTEN STYLE LOCK — PREMIUM EDITORIAL EXPLAINER
==================================================

Use this written style specification independently for EVERY image:
Premium editorial explainer illustration for a modern educational YouTube video, 16:9 horizontal. High-quality sophisticated 2D illustration, crisp vector-like shapes, refined controlled linework, layered flat colors with subtle depth, tasteful limited palette, optional subtle paper grain, strong visual hierarchy, adult informative magazine-infographic aesthetic, clearly designed for this specific idea, not childish, not generic clipart, not a repeated template, not glossy 3D, not Pixar, not clay, not anime, not photorealistic.

INDIVIDUALITY HARD LOCK:
- Create a fresh composition for this image.
- Do not imitate or reuse the layout, perspective, character placement, background, architecture, dominant shapes, or palette of any previous image unless the content itself requires continuity.
- Do not use any previous generated image as a visual reference.
- Do not repeat a mascot or recurring character without a real narrative reason.
- Vary composition mode between editorial hero illustration, explainer diagram, narrative mini scene, map/spatial explainer, and conceptual metaphor as appropriate.
- The image must feel intentionally art-directed for this exact spoken idea.

HARD NEGATIVES:
no generic AI template look, no stock clipart collage, no repetitive center-character-plus-icons formula, no mandatory countryball mascot, no stick-figure style, no glossy 3D, no Pixar, no clay, no anime, no photorealistic stock-photo aesthetic, no watermark.

==================================================
DEUTSCH-HARD-LOCK — JEDER SICHTBARE TEXT
==================================================

This is a GERMAN project. Every readable word visible anywhere in every generated image MUST be German.

This applies to headlines, map labels, signs, legends, diagrams, callouts, calendars, interface-like elements and background text.
Do NOT generate English labels. Do NOT generate pseudo-text or fake writing.
If text is not explicitly needed, generate NO TEXT.
Any English visible text = HARD FAIL and regenerate.
Any pseudo-text = HARD FAIL and regenerate.
