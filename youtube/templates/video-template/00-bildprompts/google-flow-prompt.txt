==================================================
WRITTEN STYLE LOCK — PREMIUM EDITORIAL EXPLAINER
==================================================

Use this written style specification independently for EVERY image:
Premium editorial explainer illustration for a modern educational YouTube video, 16:9 horizontal. High-quality sophisticated 2D illustration with confident editorial art direction, crisp vector-like shapes, refined controlled linework, layered flat colors with subtle depth, tasteful rich-but-controlled palette, selective texture, intentional lighting accents, strong hierarchy and a clear focal idea. Adult educational magazine / documentary-infographic aesthetic. The image should feel authored, specific and visually alive rather than sterile, generic or templated. Not childish, not stock clipart, not a repeated composition, not glossy 3D, not Pixar, not clay, not anime, not photorealistic.

==================================================
VISUAL STORYTELLING HARD LOCK
==================================================

Every image must communicate a visual story, relationship, tension, change, cause/effect, comparison or spatial idea — not merely display nouns from the narration.

When the content allows it:
- prefer a scene with direction, interaction or consequence over a static centered object;
- use foreground, midground and background to create controlled depth;
- use diagonals, leading lines, scale contrast, framing, overlap or asymmetry to create visual energy;
- make the viewer immediately understand what is happening and why it matters;
- show cause and effect visibly when the narration describes cause and effect;
- use human/editorial figures only when they improve the explanation, never as filler;
- let lighting, contrast and palette support the emotional or historical tone of the sentence.

==================================================
ANTI-LIFELESS HARD LOCK
==================================================

Reject and regenerate an image if it looks flat, empty, dull, sterile or like a generic AI explainer card.

Default failures include:
- one small centered object floating on a blank background;
- a centered icon with two decorative icons beside it;
- repeated clock/map/object layouts with only labels changed;
- excessive empty pastel background without narrative purpose;
- lifeless front-on compositions with no depth, direction or focal tension;
- generic icon collage / presentation-slide look;
- decorative elements that do not explain anything;
- the same composition mode in adjacent images without a real narrative reason.

Clean does NOT mean empty. Minimal does NOT mean lifeless. Simplicity must still have composition, depth, tension and visual intention.

==================================================
INDIVIDUALITY HARD LOCK
==================================================

- Create a fresh composition for every Bild NN.
- Do not imitate or reuse the layout, perspective, character placement, background structure, architecture, dominant shapes, or palette of any previous image unless the content itself genuinely requires continuity.
- Do not use any previous generated image as a visual reference.
- Do not repeat a mascot or recurring character without a real narrative reason.
- Vary composition modes intentionally: editorial hero, narrative mini scene, historical editorial scene, map/spatial explainer, conceptual metaphor, explainer diagram.
- Adjacent images should normally change composition mode, camera angle, spatial arrangement or palette emphasis.
- Each image must feel specifically art-directed for the exact spoken idea.
