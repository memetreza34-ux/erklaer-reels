==================================================
WRITTEN STYLE LOCK — PREMIUM EDITORIAL EXPLAINER
==================================================

Premium editorial explainer illustration for a modern educational YouTube documentary, 16:9 horizontal. Sophisticated high-quality 2D editorial illustration, crisp vector-like shapes, refined controlled linework, layered flat colors with subtle depth, selective paper/print texture, intentional light and shadow accents, strong hierarchy and a clear visual story. Adult documentary-magazine aesthetic. Historical scenes should feel authored and spatially believable without becoming photorealistic. Maps should be elegant editorial maps integrated into the story rather than sterile atlas screenshots. No glossy 3D, no Pixar, no clay, no anime, no photorealism.

==================================================
VISUAL STORYTELLING HARD LOCK
==================================================

Do not merely place nouns from the narration on screen. Show the relationship, consequence, conflict, transition or spatial idea.

When useful:
- use foreground, midground and background;
- use diagonals, leading lines, overlap, scale contrast and asymmetry;
- make cause and effect visible;
- use restrained editorial human figures for historical scenes when people improve understanding;
- use maps only when geography itself is the explanation;
- use different palette emphasis for pre-1945, wartime, Soviet era, 1991 and present day;
- prefer a narrative scene over a generic card when both can explain the same idea.

==================================================
ANTI-LIFELESS HARD LOCK
==================================================

Avoid flat, empty, generic, sterile or presentation-slide-like results before generation.

Hard failures to prevent in the prompt itself:
- one small centered object floating on blank background;
- generic icon collage;
- repeated Baltic map with only labels changed;
- excessive empty pastel space;
- front-on composition with no depth or directional energy;
- repeated composition mode in adjacent images without narrative reason;
- decorative props that do not explain the narration.

Clean does NOT mean empty. Minimal does NOT mean lifeless. Non-cover images still receive only one generation.
