==================================================
WRITTEN STYLE LOCK — SERIOUS MINIMAL COUNTRYBALL
==================================================

Serious Minimal Countryball Explainer visual world, exactly matching the active Reel visual DNA, adapted only to a 16:9 horizontal YouTube canvas. Clean serious minimal 2D countryball explainer. Perfectly round ball characters when an actor is useful, simple white eyes, thick clean black outlines, flat controlled colors, minimal soft graphic shading, low-to-medium controlled detail, one dominant focal subject, zero to three meaningful supporting props, simple solid or muted background with optional subtle paper texture or gradient, strong symbolic storytelling, adult and informative, never childish or goofy.

Historical and geopolitical content must remain in this reduced visual language. Translate wars, conferences, migration and border changes into countryballs, simple maps, arrows, documents, trains, borders and symbols. Do NOT create realistic humans, realistic crowds, cinematic battle paintings, detailed conference-room people, photorealistic cities or realistic skin/hands.

If a countryball actor is used:
- perfectly round body
- simple white eyes
- thick clean black outline
- restrained expression
- tiny simple limbs only when needed
- no hair, neck, realistic hands or human face

If a map, object or symbol explains the idea more clearly, no countryball is required.

==================================================
VISUAL STORYTELLING HARD LOCK
==================================================

Stay minimal, but make the relationship instantly understandable. Use simple arrows, routes, borders, scale contrast, split maps, documents and a few purposeful props. Vary placement and arrangement while keeping exactly the same Countryball visual DNA.

==================================================
ANTI-LIFELESS HARD LOCK
==================================================

Avoid tiny isolated objects lost in huge empty backgrounds, meaningless icon collages and the exact same centered-countryball composition on every image. Use the canvas deliberately, but do not add realistic detail merely to make it busy.

Clean does NOT mean empty. Minimal does NOT mean lifeless.
