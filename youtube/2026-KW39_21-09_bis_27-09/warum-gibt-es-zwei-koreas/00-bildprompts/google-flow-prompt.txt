WRITTEN STYLE LOCK — PREMIUM EDITORIAL EXPLAINER
==================================================

Premium editorial explainer illustration for a modern educational YouTube documentary, 16:9 horizontal. High-quality sophisticated 2D illustration with confident art direction, crisp vector-like shapes, refined controlled linework, layered flat colors with subtle depth, rich but disciplined color palettes, selective paper/print texture, intentional lighting accents, strong hierarchy and a clear focal story. Adult educational magazine / documentary-infographic aesthetic. Every image must feel authored for its exact historical or geopolitical idea, visually alive and memorable, never sterile or templated. No glossy 3D, no Pixar, no clay, no anime, no photorealism.

==================================================
VISUAL STORYTELLING HARD LOCK
==================================================

Do not merely place nouns from the narration on screen. Show the relationship, consequence, conflict, transition or spatial idea.

When useful:
- use foreground, midground and background for depth;
- use diagonals, leading lines, overlap, scale contrast, framing and asymmetry;
- make cause and effect visible;
- use expressive but restrained editorial figures when people improve the historical story;
- use maps only when the geography itself is the explanation;
- use lighting and palette to separate eras, political systems and military phases;
- prefer a narrative or conceptual scene over a static infographic card when both can explain the same point.

==================================================
ANTI-LIFELESS HARD LOCK
==================================================

Reject and regenerate any image that feels flat, empty, generic, sterile or like a presentation slide.

Hard failures:
- one small centered object floating on a blank background;
- generic icon collage;
- repeated peninsula map with only labels changed;
- excessive empty pastel space without narrative purpose;
- front-on composition with no depth, direction or focal tension;
- repeated composition mode in adjacent images without a real reason;
- decorative props that do not explain the narration.

Clean does NOT mean empty. Minimal does NOT mean lifeless.

==================================================
INDIVIDUALITY HARD LOCK
==================================================

- Create a fresh composition for every Bild NN.
- Do not imitate or reuse the layout, perspective, object placement, background structure, architecture, dominant shapes or palette arrangement of the previous image unless continuity is genuinely needed.

- Rotate intentionally between: editorial hero, historical narrative scene, map/spatial explainer, conceptual metaphor, military movement map, political contrast and documentary system illustration.
- Adjacent images should normally change angle, scale, composition mode and palette emphasis.

HARD NEGATIVES:
no mandatory mascot, no countryball template, no stick figures, no childish cartoon look, no cheap clipart, no generic AI template, no empty presentation-card aesthetic, no glossy 3D, no Pixar, no clay, no anime, no photorealistic stock-photo look, no watermark.
