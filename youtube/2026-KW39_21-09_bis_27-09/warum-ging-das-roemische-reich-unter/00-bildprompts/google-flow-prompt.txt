==================================================

Use this exact visual DNA for every image:
Universal Editorial Stickman World v1.2, 16:9 horizontal, premium clean digital 2D editorial illustration, every visible human including tiny background people is a minimalist stickman with a round or slightly oval head, tiny black dot eyes, minimal mouth, thin simplified arms and legs, strongly simplified body, historical clothing only as simplified outer shapes over the stickman anatomy, richer believable environments with natural lighting and subtle 2D shading, moderate-to-vivid scene-appropriate colors, crisp modern editorial composition, clear depth, one strong visual purpose.

HARD NEGATIVES:
no beige/sepia color wash, no parchment texture, no antique manuscript look, no washed-out pastel default, no old textbook-map aesthetic, no normal cartoon humans, no semi-realistic humans, no detailed realistic faces, no mixed human styles, no photorealism, no 3D, no Pixar, no clay, no countryballs, no watermark.

Every visible person MUST remain a stickman. If even a small background person becomes a normal cartoon or semi-realistic person, the image is a STYLE FAIL and must be regenerated.

Environments may be richer and more detailed than the humans. Use clear modern colors, natural light, convincing simplified architecture, terrain, objects and materials. Beige/brown may occur locally where historically sensible but must never dominate the whole visual world.

DEUTSCH-HARD-LOCK — JEDER SICHTBARE TEXT
==================================================

This is a GERMAN project. Every readable word visible anywhere in every generated image MUST be German.

This applies to:
- headlines
- map labels
- oceans and seas
- city/region labels
- signs
- legends
- diagrams
- arrows/callouts
- calendars
- UI-like elements
- background text

Do NOT automatically generate English map labels. Do NOT generate pseudo-text or fake writing.
If text is not explicitly needed, generate NO TEXT rather than inventing labels.
Any English visible text = HARD FAIL and the image must be regenerated.
Any pseudo-text/unreadable text = HARD FAIL and must be removed or regenerated.

Examples:

MEDITERRANEAN SEA = forbidden → MITTELMEER
