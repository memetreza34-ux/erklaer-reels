WRITTEN STYLE LOCK — SERIOUS MINIMAL COUNTRYBALL
==================================================
Serious Minimal Countryball Explainer visual world, exactly matching the active Reel visual DNA and adapted only to horizontal 16:9. Clean, serious, premium flat 2D illustration. Perfectly round Countryball actors, simple white eyes, thick clean black outlines, controlled flat colors, restrained soft graphic shading, adult and informative, never childish or goofy.

ABSOLUTE ACTOR LOCK:
- ONLY Countryball-style figures may act as people or groups.
- NO normal illustrated humans.
- NO humanoid cartoon people.
- NO human silhouettes.
- NO human hands, arms, heads, faces, bodies or crowds.
- If a scene normally needs a person, replace that person with a Countryball actor or symbolic object.
- Buildings, documents, maps, streets, rooms and objects may appear, but human figures may not.

No photorealism. No realistic human scenes. No glossy 3D. No Pixar. No clay. No anime.

==================================================
SCENE ILLUSTRATION V2 — HARD LOCK
==================================================
THIS VIDEO MUST LOOK LIKE A SERIES OF CLEAR ILLUSTRATED SCENES, NOT LIKE ABSTRACT INFOGRAPHIC POSTERS.

Prefer:
- a recognizable place or situation: courthouse, town square, parliament exterior, market street, school entrance, road, bridge, voting place, library, public square, home/property scene, city street
- Countryballs visibly doing something inside that place
- one clear action or relationship per image
- colorful but controlled flat 2D illustration
- visible environmental context around the main idea
- simple depth through foreground, midground and background
- scene-specific props integrated naturally into the environment

Avoid as a default:
- abstract symbol boards
- presentation-slide layouts
- split-panel infographic posters
- multiple boxed explanations
- rows of icons
- giant diagram arrows floating in empty space
- tiny Countryballs surrounded by labels
- beige/navy monotony across many consecutive images
- one static object centered on an empty background

Use infographic structure ONLY when the spoken idea genuinely cannot be shown as a concrete scene. Even then, keep it to one relationship, not a dashboard.

==================================================
COLOR & ILLUSTRATION QUALITY — HARD LOCK
==================================================
The video should feel more vivid, approachable and visually rich while staying in the same Countryball world.

- Use a broader scene-based palette across the video: sky blue, warm cream, soft green, terracotta, muted red, mustard yellow, teal and navy may all appear when appropriate.
- Do NOT make every image beige + dark blue.
- Each image should still have one dominant palette with 1–3 supporting accents.
- Consecutive images should normally vary background hue and environment when the narration changes.
- Buildings and environments should be simplified but attractive illustrated forms, not sterile symbols.
- Countryballs remain the cleanest, most visually dominant actors.
- Text must support the scene, not turn the scene into a poster.

==================================================
PREMIUM DESIGN LAYER V1 — HARD LOCK
==================================================
Premium means BETTER DESIGN INSIDE THE SAME COUNTRYBALL WORLD, never a style change.
Use clear visual hierarchy, meaningful scale contrast, deliberate 16:9 balance, purposeful overlaps, elegant flat foreground/midground/background layering when useful, intentional negative space and clean typography.
Prefer visual relationships over object lists. Avoid random centering, weak empty backgrounds, generic icon grids and repeated layouts.
Clean does NOT mean empty. Minimal does NOT mean lifeless. Premium does NOT mean realistic.

==================================================
VISUAL STORYTELLING HARD LOCK
==================================================
Show the spoken idea through an action or situation whenever possible:
- freedom = Countryball choosing or moving freely
- state power = institution physically setting or enforcing a boundary
- rights = legal protection inside a concrete scene
- equality before law = different Countryballs entering the same courthouse process
- opportunity = bridge, school, road or access becoming usable
- market freedom = Countryballs buying, selling or choosing between stalls
- conflict between freedoms = two paths meeting at one shared boundary

Every image must communicate one clear idea within about one second.

==================================================
ADAPTIVE IMAGE DENSITY V3 — HARD LOCK
==================================================
There is NO fixed target image count as a global rule. This project contains 32 images because the narration has 32 distinct visual purposes. One image = one clear visual purpose. Dense ideas are split instead of overloaded. Preferred average hold is about 4.5–7.5 seconds. No filler images. Complex information should be split across multiple elegant images rather than compressed into one overloaded frame.
