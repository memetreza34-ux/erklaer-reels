==================================================

Apply this written style specification independently to EVERY image:
Premium editorial explainer illustration for a modern educational YouTube documentary, 16:9 horizontal. ==================================================
VISUAL STORYTELLING HARD LOCK
==================================================

Do not merely place nouns from the narration on screen. Show relationships, consequences, transitions and spatial ideas.

When appropriate:
- use foreground, midground and background for depth;
- use diagonals, leading lines, overlap, scale contrast, framing and asymmetry for visual energy;
- make cause and effect visible;
- use restrained editorial figures when people improve the story;
- use lighting and palette to support historical period, confusion or resolution;
- prefer a narrative or conceptual scene over a static infographic card when it explains better.

==================================================
ANTI-LIFELESS HARD LOCK
==================================================

Reject and regenerate any image that feels flat, empty, generic, sterile or like a presentation slide.

Hard failures:
- one small centered object floating on a blank background;
- icon + two decorative icons formula;
- repeated clock/map layouts with only labels changed;
- excessive empty pastel space without narrative purpose;
- front-on composition with no depth, direction or focal tension;
- generic icon collage;
- repeated composition mode in adjacent images without a real reason;
- decorative props that do not explain the narration.

Clean does NOT mean empty. Minimal does NOT mean lifeless.
