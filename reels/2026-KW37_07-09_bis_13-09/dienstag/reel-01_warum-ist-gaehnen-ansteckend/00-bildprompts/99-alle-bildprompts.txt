# ALLE BILDPROMPTS — Warum ist Gähnen ansteckend?

Format: 9:16 · Serious Minimal Countryball Explainer · Adaptive Dense V2 · 21 Bildmomente

## VERBINDLICHER WORLD-LOCK FÜR ALLE 21 BILDER

Der KI-Agent muss vor Bild 01 **einmal** diese Welt festsetzen und sie für das komplette Reel beibehalten.

**Fixed visual world:** `serious-minimal-countryball-explainer`

Create every image in ONE fixed clean, serious, minimal 2D countryball-style explainer universe for vertical 9:16 Reels.

CORE LOOK:
- thick clean black outlines
- flat controlled 2D colours
- very light simple graphic shading
- low-to-medium detail
- large perfectly round countryball-like characters when actors are needed
- simple white eyes and restrained facial expressions
- no normal illustrated humans
- backgrounds mostly solid or muted colour fields, subtle gradients or light texture
- short bold German text only when explicitly requested
- strong smartphone readability
- one dominant visual idea per image

Do NOT make every image only a ball on an empty background. When the explanation benefits, add 1 to 3 meaningful supporting elements such as a phone, brain symbol, book/page, research paper, simple screen, TMS coil, heart symbol, spotlight or other relevant prop. Supporting elements must explain the spoken idea, never just decorate the frame.

Use three composition modes naturally across the Reel:
1. minimal-symbolic
2. supported-explainer
3. simple-mini-scene

A simple mini-scene may include one reduced contextual element such as a chair, table, door or screen, but NEVER a detailed realistic room.

FLAG RULE: use country flags only when geography, politics, nationality or cultural identity matters. This Reel is general psychology/science, so use neutral solid-colour balls only.

STRICTLY FORBIDDEN: normal illustrated humans, generic editorial people, stick figures, photorealism, realistic hands/skin/faces, realistic detailed interiors, photo backgrounds, mixed media, anime, clay, glossy 3D/Pixar, detailed medical 3D anatomy, stock-photo aesthetics, tiny decorative balls, random tongue/gimmick faces, busy icon collages, UI boards, logos, watermarks and pseudo-writing.

Visible text is German only. If no text is requested below, generate no readable text.
