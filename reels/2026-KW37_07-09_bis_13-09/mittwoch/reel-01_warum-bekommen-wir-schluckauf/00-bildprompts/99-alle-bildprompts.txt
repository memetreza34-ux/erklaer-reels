## VERBINDLICHER WORLD-LOCK FÜR ALLE 21 BILDER

Vor Bild 01 diese eine Bildwelt einmal festsetzen und danach für das komplette Reel unverändert beibehalten.

**Fixed visual world:** `serious-minimal-countryball-explainer`

Create every image in ONE fixed clean, serious, minimal 2D countryball-style explainer universe for vertical 9:16 Reels.

CORE LOOK:
- thick clean black outlines
- flat controlled 2D colours
- very light soft graphic shading
- low-to-medium detail
- large perfectly round countryball-like characters only when an actor helps
- simple white eyes, restrained readable expressions
- no normal illustrated humans
- backgrounds mainly solid or muted colour fields, subtle gradients or very light texture
- strong smartphone readability
- one dominant visual idea per image
- use 0 to 3 meaningful supporting objects when they improve the explanation
- alternate naturally between minimal-symbolic, supported-explainer and simple-mini-scene compositions

Do NOT turn every frame into one ball on an empty background. Use simple props and mechanisms such as a diaphragm curve, airflow, throat/glottis shape, nerve path, plate, glass, bubbles, thermometer contrast, clock, water glass or simple medical cue when they directly explain the spoken idea. Keep them in the exact same flat 2D outline language.

FLAG RULE: this is a general body/science topic, so all characters are neutral solid-colour balls. No country flags.

STRICTLY FORBIDDEN: normal illustrated humans, human-headed characters, stick figures, photorealism, realistic skin or organs, detailed realistic rooms, photo backgrounds, mixed media, anime, clay, glossy 3D/Pixar, realistic medical renders, stock-photo aesthetics, tiny decorative balls, random tongues or gag faces, busy icon collages, floating UI boards, logos, watermarks and pseudo-writing.

Visible text is German only and only when explicitly requested below. If no text is requested, generate no readable text.
