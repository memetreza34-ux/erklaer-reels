# ALLE BILDPROMPTS — Was ist Föderalismus?

Format: 9:16 · Serious Minimal Countryball Explainer · Adaptive Dense V2 · 20 Bildmomente

## VERBINDLICHER WORLD-LOCK FÜR ALLE 20 BILDER

Vor Bild 01 diese eine Bildwelt einmal festsetzen und danach für das komplette Reel unverändert beibehalten.

**Fixed visual world:** `serious-minimal-countryball-explainer`

Create every image in ONE fixed clean, serious, minimal 2D countryball-style explainer universe for vertical 9:16 Reels.

CORE LOOK:
- thick, clean black outlines with consistent line weight
- flat, controlled 2D colours with only light graphic shading
- low-to-medium detail, never empty but never cluttered
- perfectly round countryball-like actors when a country, state or political actor is useful
- simple white eyes, restrained serious expressions, no goofy facial gimmicks
- flags and flag colours are allowed here because geography and political identity are directly relevant
- maps, borders, documents, ballot boxes, parliament buildings, school objects, police symbols, arrows and simple institutional props may be used when they strengthen the spoken idea
- backgrounds should usually be solid or muted colour fields, subtle gradients, simplified map textures or very simple contextual environments
- one dominant visual idea per image, supported by 0 to 3 meaningful elements
- strong smartphone readability within about one second
- naturally alternate between `minimal-symbolic`, `supported-explainer` and `simple-mini-scene`

IMPORTANT:
Do NOT make every frame only a ball on an empty background. Political and geographic explanations should use the visual strengths of this world: countryballs, Germany map silhouettes, Bundesland divisions, government buildings, law documents, arrows, border lines, school objects and institutional symbols. Every supporting element must have a direct explanatory purpose.

CONSISTENCY RULE:
The topic, camera angle, background colour and props may change. The art world may not change. Keep the same line weight, eye design, round geometry, flat colour logic, restrained shading, typography treatment and seriousness across all 20 images.

STRICTLY FORBIDDEN:
normal illustrated humans, humanoid cartoon people, stick figures, photorealism, realistic faces or hands, realistic detailed interiors, photography, mixed media, anime, manga, clay, glossy 3D/Pixar, stock-photo aesthetics, childish mascot posing, random tongue faces, tiny decorative balls, busy UI dashboards, giant icon clouds, watermarks, logos that are not necessary to explain the institution, and pseudo-writing.

TEXT RULE:
Visible text is German only and only when explicitly requested below. Use bold, clean, high-contrast uppercase sans-serif lettering with a strong outline when text is requested. If no text is requested, generate no readable text.

## PRODUKTIONSABLAUF FÜR FLOW

Streng seriell arbeiten:

`Bild 01 erzeugen → vollständig warten → Inhalt + Bildwelt prüfen → exakt als Bild 01.png benennen → ablegen → Ablage prüfen → erst dann Bild 02`
