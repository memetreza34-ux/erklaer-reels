GLOBAL REEL STYLE LOCK – MANDATORY FOR EVERY IMAGE
Use the fixed Reel visual world "Clarity-First Editorial Reel". Keep the technical compatibility style ID `modern-countryball-explainer`, but execute it with the following rules:

- vertical 9:16, smartphone-first
- clean hand-drawn 2D editorial cartoon
- thick slightly organic black outlines
- simplified readable shapes
- flat or lightly cel-shaded colors
- subtle soft shadows only
- no more than a faint paper/grain texture
- low to medium detail density
- one dominant visual idea and one clear physical action
- only one to three supporting elements
- the core meaning should be understandable in about one second
- simple clear backgrounds; environment only when it helps the explanation
- lighting normally bright, clear and graphic, not dark cinematic concept art
- simple cartoon people, anthropomorphic objects, concrete objects/mechanisms or countryball-like characters may be used depending on what communicates the idea fastest
- countryballs are NOT mandatory for general topics
- technical cutaways, engineering schematics, blueprint aesthetics and highly detailed machinery are NOT the default visual world
- the topic may change the subject matter but never the visual world
- avoid icon boards, floating reaction cards, UI boxes, speech-bubble rings, repeated centered-character-plus-icons layouts and decorative clutter
- no photorealism, realistic humans, anime, manga, clay, glossy 3D, Pixar look, stock-photo look or painterly concept art
- never borrow the separate YouTube stick-figure/16:9 visual world
- prompts are English; visible image text is German only
- if no exact text is requested, show no readable text
- no logos, watermarks, pseudo-text, workflow labels, image numbers or filenames inside any generated image
