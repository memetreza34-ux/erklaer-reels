GLOBAL REEL STYLE LOCK – MANDATORY FOR EVERY IMAGE
Use exactly ONE fixed Reel visual world: "Modern Countryball Explainer". Never create or switch to any second visual world during this Reel.

- vertical 9:16, smartphone-first
- clean flat 2D vector cartoon, modern minimalist countryball-inspired style
- thick confident black outlines
- simplified readable forms and low-to-medium detail
- flat or very lightly shaded colors, subtle soft shadows
- calm solid colour background or lightly textured flat colour
- normally bright, clear and graphic lighting, not cinematic concept art
- when an ACTOR appears, it must be a round countryball-style character: a clean perfect circle or sphere body with NO separate head, simple expressive white eyes with black pupils, and at most small simple arms, hands or feet when an action requires them
- ball bodies must stay geometrically round and must never become bean-shaped, oval, egg-shaped, human-headed or humanoid
- use flag patterns on the ball only when geographic identity actually matters; otherwise use neutral solid-colour balls
- an actor is NOT required in every image; if an object, mechanism, plant, document, landscape or physical process explains the point more clearly, use that instead
- objects shown without a ball character must keep exactly the same thick outline weight, flat colour treatment and simplified 2D shape language
- do NOT add a decorative ball just to satisfy the visual style
- NEVER use human heads on ball bodies, humanoid cartoon people or stick figures
- countries, institutions or historical actors must be represented with flag-marked or neutral balls together with maps, borders, documents or buildings
- one dominant visual idea and one clear physical action per image
- only one to three supporting elements
- the core meaning should be understandable in about one second
- simple contextual backgrounds; no overloaded interiors
- technical cutaways, engineering schematics, blueprint aesthetics and highly detailed machinery are not the default visual world
- the topic changes only the subject matter, never the visual world
- no topic-specific sub-world for science, rain, biology, history, aviation, medicine or any other theme
- avoid icon boards, floating reaction cards, UI boxes, speech-bubble rings and repeated centered-character-plus-icons layouts
- no photorealistic humans, anime, manga, clay, glossy 3D, Pixar look, stock-photo look or painterly concept art
- never borrow the separate YouTube stick-figure/16:9 visual world
- prompts are English; visible image text is German only
- if no exact text is requested, show no readable text
- no logos, watermarks, pseudo-text, workflow labels, image numbers or filenames inside generated images
