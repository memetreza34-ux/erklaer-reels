VERBINDLICHE REEL-BILDWELT – GILT FÜR JEDES BILD
Create a vertical 9:16 educational explainer illustration in the fixed Human Editorial Explainer visual world. Use one coherent visual language across the entire reel: clean hand-drawn 2D editorial cartoon, confident black outlines, simplified readable anatomy, flat or lightly cel-shaded colors, subtle soft shadows, very light paper/grain texture, clear foreground-midground-background separation, and one instantly readable physical story moment per image.

Whenever a person appears, the person must be recognizably HUMAN: a normal human head shape, neck, torso, arms, hands and legs when the framing needs them. Depending on the scene, show a head, portrait, upper body, hands or full body. Faces should be simple but clearly human, with eyes, brows, nose, mouth and hairstyle when useful. Never turn people into balls, spheres, countryballs, mascot heads or stick figures.

A human is NOT mandatory in every image. If the explanation is clearer through architecture, an object, mechanism, environment or close-up, let that physical subject carry the scene. Build a concrete mini-scene first; do not build an icon board.

For this reel, keep one coherent medieval design system across all images: warm sandstone walls, terracotta/red tiled roofs, muted ochre and parchment beige, moss green accents, deep navy for night, charcoal/dark clothing for attackers, warm lantern/firelight. Keep the same simplified medieval architecture throughout.

Use one recurring city defender whenever a defender needs to return: a simplified recognizably human medieval guard wearing a red-and-cream tunic with a small white castle emblem, simple brown hair, expressive human face and practical medieval clothing. Attackers are simplified human soldiers in charcoal/dark tunics with simple steel helmets. Merchants, civilians and workers are simplified human figures in muted earth-tone clothing. No national flags on clothing.

IMPORTANT STYLE CONTINUITY RULE
Bild 00 establishes the reel-specific visual anchor. From Bild 01 onward, preserve Bild 00's line thickness, shading level, texture strength, stone style, roof style, human face style, body proportions, clothing simplification and overall color treatment. Do not copy the exact composition; keep the visual DNA consistent while varying camera angle and scene.

COMPOSITION RULES
- Prefer concrete physical storytelling over abstract symbols.
- One dominant action or visual idea per image.
- Use one to three supporting elements only.
- Vary framing: wide establishing shot, medium action shot, close-up detail, low angle, slight top-down, night scene.
- Do not repeat a centered-character-plus-icons template.
- No floating reaction cards, no icon rings, no UI boxes, no infographic boards, no split-screen unless explicitly requested.
- Backgrounds should feel like simplified locations, not empty color fields when a location helps the story.
- Full 9:16 composition; no artificial subtitle-safe empty band.

TEXT RULES
Visible text is German only and only when explicitly requested in the current image section. Use the exact requested phrase once. Never duplicate the headline. Never add English visible text, pseudo-text, logos, watermarks, workflow labels, image numbers, filenames or technical labels.

FORBIDDEN
No countryballs, no spherical mascot people, no flag-painted balls, no stick figures, no photorealistic people, no realistic photo faces, no anime, no manga, no clay, no glossy 3D, no cinematic realism, no Pixar-like rendering, no 16:9 YouTube visual world, no Ink-Explainer-like longform style.

=== BILD 00 — COVER ===
Create the visual anchor for the reel. Show a fortified medieval city behind a massive warm-sandstone wall with two strong towers and one heavy wooden gate. View the wall from slightly below so it feels powerful and protective. Inside the wall, show a compact cluster of terracotta-roofed houses and one taller civic/church-like tower. On the battlement, place the recurring human city defender: a simplified recognizably human medieval guard in a red-and-cream tunic with a small white castle emblem, normal human head and body proportions, expressive face, simple brown hair. Outside the wall in the lower foreground, show only a few simplified human attackers in dark charcoal clothing and simple steel helmets, with one spear, one torch and one wooden ladder. Make the contrast instantly clear: safe city above/inside, danger below/outside. Keep the composition clean, not crowded. Add exactly one large bold German headline at the bottom: "WARUM HATTEN STÄDTE MAUERN?" No other readable text.

=== BILD 01 — STADT ALS GESCHLOSSENER RAUM ===
Create a wide elevated establishing shot of the same medieval city completely enclosed by its stone wall. Show the wall forming a clear ring around tightly grouped houses, a market lane, a few trees and one central tower. Outside the wall: open fields and one road leading to the main gate. Use only a few tiny simplified human residents for scale. The wall itself is the dominant subject. Morning light, warm parchment sky, muted earthy palette. Add exactly one short German text near the lower third: "MEHR ALS SCHUTZ" No other readable text.

=== BILD 02 — ANGRIFF WIRD GESTOPPT ===
Show a real siege moment from outside the wall. In the foreground, three or four simplified human attackers in dark tunics and steel helmets approach with a ladder and spears. The ladder reaches toward the wall, but the tall stone barrier dominates the scene and clearly blocks them. On top of the battlement, the recurring human city defender and two smaller human defenders look down from safety. Use a diagonal composition from attackers low in the foreground to defenders high above. Add exactly one bold German text at the bottom: "SCHUTZ VOR ANGRIFFEN" No other readable text.

=== BILD 03 — KONTROLLE AM STADTTOR ===
Show the main gate as a practical checkpoint, not as an abstract symbol. Use a three-quarter angle on the open stone arch. A simplified human merchant in earth-tone medieval clothing arrives with a small wooden cart carrying sacks, a barrel and one crate. A simplified human guard stands at the gate and physically checks the cargo while the city street is visible beyond the doors. A second human traveler waits behind on the road. Keep the gate architecture large and important in the frame. Add exactly one bold German text at the bottom: "KONTROLLE AM TOR" No other readable text.

=== BILD 04 — ABGABEN UND ZÖLLE ===
Create a closer crop at the same gate. Show the human merchant handing two or three coins directly to a human gate official. Their hands and faces should be visible enough to make the exchange immediately clear. The cart, sack and barrel remain partly visible so the trade context is obvious. Keep warm ochre and sandstone tones. Add exactly one short German text: "ABGABEN AM TOR" No other readable text.

The city wall should still match the same stone and roof design from Bild 00. Add exactly one bold German text at the bottom: "NACHTS TOR ZU" No other readable text.
