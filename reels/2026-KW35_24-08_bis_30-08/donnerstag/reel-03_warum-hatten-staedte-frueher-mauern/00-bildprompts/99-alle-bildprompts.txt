VERBINDLICHE REEL-BILDWELT – GILT FÜR JEDES BILD
Create a vertical 9:16 scene-first educational explainer illustration in a modern countryball-inspired editorial cartoon world. The visual language must stay identical across the entire reel: clean hand-drawn 2D vector-cartoon hybrid, thick slightly organic black outlines, simple readable geometry, flat cel-like color blocks, subtle soft shading, very light paper/grain texture, strong foreground-midground-background separation, and one instantly readable physical story moment per image.

The look must feel like a polished German short-form explainer account, not a meme and not a generic infographic. Build a real mini-scene first. Architecture, doors, roads, walls, tools, carts, coins, ladders, cannons, buildings and landscapes should physically tell the story. Use round cartoon characters only when an actor is needed. Characters have expressive white eyes, tiny simple limbs only when useful, and no realistic human anatomy.

For this reel, keep one coherent medieval design system across all images: warm sandstone walls, terracotta/red tiled roofs, muted ochre and parchment beige, moss green accents, deep navy for night, charcoal attackers, warm lantern/firelight. Keep the same simplified medieval architecture and the same recurring city-defender design whenever it returns: a red-and-cream round city character with a small white castle emblem and determined white eyes. Attackers use charcoal/dark round bodies with simple steel helmets. Merchants and civilians use muted earth-tone bodies without national flags.

IMPORTANT STYLE CONTINUITY RULE
Bild 00 establishes the reel-specific visual anchor. From Bild 01 onward, preserve Bild 00's line thickness, shading level, texture strength, stone style, roof style, character eye style, city-emblem design and overall color treatment. Do not copy the exact composition; keep the visual DNA consistent while varying camera angle and scene.

COMPOSITION RULES
- Prefer concrete physical storytelling over abstract symbols.
- One dominant action or visual idea per image.
- Use one to three supporting elements only.
- Vary framing: wide establishing shot, medium action shot, close-up detail, low angle, slight top-down, night scene.
- Do not repeat a centered-character-plus-icons template.
- No floating reaction cards, no icon rings, no UI boxes, no infographic boards, no split-screen unless explicitly requested.
- Backgrounds should feel like simplified locations, not empty color fields when a location helps the story.
- Full 9:16 composition; no artificial subtitle-safe empty band.

TEXT RULES
Visible text is German only and only when explicitly requested in the current image section. Use the exact requested phrase once. Never duplicate the headline. Never add English visible text, pseudo-text, logos, watermarks, workflow labels, image numbers, filenames or technical labels.

FORBIDDEN
No realistic humans, no photorealism, no anime, no manga, no clay, no glossy 3D, no cinematic realism, no thin-line stick figures, no 16:9 YouTube visual world, no Ink-Explainer-like longform style.

On the battlement, place the recurring red-and-cream city-defender ball with the small white castle emblem and determined eyes. Outside the wall in the lower foreground, show only a few charcoal attacker balls with simple steel helmets, one spear, one torch and one wooden ladder. Make the contrast instantly clear: safe city above/inside, danger below/outside. Keep the composition clean, not crowded. Add exactly one large bold German headline at the bottom: "WARUM HATTEN STÄDTE MAUERN?" No other readable text.

Add exactly one short German text near the lower third: "MEHR ALS SCHUTZ" No other readable text.
